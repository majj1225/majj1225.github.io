Research

My research interests are geometry and topology. My current work focuses on developing holomorphic curve invariants of Legendrian and Lagrangian submanifolds and studying their algebraic structures. A central applicaton is the classification of exact Lagrangian fillings of Legendrian links, where these invariants reveal profound connections to cluster theory.
